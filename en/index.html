A few useful details.

Are the products available for sale?+

The first DC motor controller prototype is complete; revisions and validation continue. The standard IMU is in commercialization preparation, while the advanced IMU is in R&D. Contact us for current availability.

Does the IMU include ready-to-use attitude estimation?+

The sensor architecture presented here is for acceleration, angular velocity and magnetic field measurements. We do not claim validated onboard sensor fusion or navigation performance.

Do the interface charts show real measurements?+

No. The animations, illustrations and interfaces on this website are concepts explaining the intended workflow. They are not substitutes for actual product photographs or test results.
